Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start my free trial?
Open the BimboBuddy app and follow the on-screen instructions. You'll get 14 days of full access, then the subscription continues at the monthly rate unless you cancel.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > BimboBuddy > Cancel Subscription.
Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > BimboBuddy > Cancel Subscription.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by the app store. For iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android, use the Google Play refund flow. Refunds are at the store's discretion.
Why isn't my location working?
Make sure Location Services are enabled for BimboBuddy and set to "While Using the App".
How do I add a baby-friendly place?
Go to the Map tab, tap the add button, and fill in the location details. You can also add photos.

Account deletion

If you’d like to delete your BimboBuddy account and associated data, please email us at telamigroup@gmail.com from the same email address you used to create your account.

In your message, include the subject “Delete BimboBuddy Account” and confirm that you want your account permanently deleted.

Once we receive your request, we will delete your account, profile, favorites, and any locations or posts you created within 30 days, except for any data we are required to retain for legal, security, or fraud‑prevention purposes. After your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to sign in or recover your data.
